About this project

This was my largest project, and it felt like a good one to invest in. Both in material and in plans.

The plans were made by Foureyes Furniture and it was the first time I used templates. And damn, templates are pretty dope.

Lessons Learned

  • Templating is awesome
  • Quality hardware can make the difference

Also, the headboard wasn't going to be the right width without it 😅
