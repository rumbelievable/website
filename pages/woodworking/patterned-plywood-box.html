About this project

Trying my hand at patterned plywood was a success, and incredibly satisfying.

Michael Alm (almfab.com) has been a patterned plywood pioneer and has taught this technique wonderfully. Even just revisiting this project to post on my website makes me want to dive into it again!

This box is one of my favorites, and I gifted it to my niece when she was born in June 2025!

Lessons Learned

  • Patterned plywood is bloody FUN
